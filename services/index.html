Cloud Migration & Sustainment

Move to the cloud. Stay there confidently.

Cloud migration is one of the highest-impact projects a business can undertake — and one of the easiest to get wrong. We plan carefully, execute cleanly, and provide ongoing sustainment so the benefits don't erode over time.

Whether you're moving a file server, a line-of-business application, or your entire on-premises infrastructure, we scope the work honestly and give you a fixed price before anything starts. No surprises mid-project.

What's included

  • Discovery & assessment — we audit your current environment and document exactly what needs to move and why.
  • Migration execution — managed cutover with rollback planning, minimising disruption to your team.
  • Security hardening — identity and access controls, network policies, and encryption configured from day one.
  • Sustainment & monitoring — ongoing support to keep your environment healthy, patched, and cost-efficient.
  • Handover documentation — everything written up so you're never dependent on us to understand your own systems.

Modern Website Deployment

Fast, secure websites that you actually own.

Most business websites are running on outdated, over-engineered platforms that require constant maintenance, patching, and security monitoring. We build modern static sites that are dramatically faster, inherently more secure, and significantly cheaper to run.

No database to get hacked. No plugin updates to forget. No $200/month hosting bill for a site that gets 50 visitors a day. Just a clean, professional web presence that does its job and stays out of your way.

What you get

  • Design & build — clean, responsive design tailored to your brand, built with modern tooling.
  • Edge deployment — hosted on a global CDN so your site loads fast for visitors anywhere in Australia and beyond.
  • Domain & DNS setup — we handle the technical side of getting your domain pointed and configured correctly.
  • Contact forms & integrations — lead capture, email routing, and third-party integrations where needed.
  • CI/CD pipeline — automatic deployments so updates go live the moment changes are pushed, no manual FTP required.
