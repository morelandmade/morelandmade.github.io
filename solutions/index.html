Moldz Mobile Mechanical

A Toowoomba-based mobile mechanic needed a professional online presence that matched the quality of their trade work — honest, direct, and easy for customers to act on.

What We Built

We delivered a fast, no-frills static site that puts the right information front and centre: services offered, credentials, and a direct call-to-action to book Jacob. No page builders, no bloated CMS — just a lean, edge-deployed site that loads instantly on mobile.

  • Mobile-first design for on-the-go customers
  • Lightweight static build — no database, no CMS overhead
  • Clear credentialing: licensed, insured, 15+ years experience
  • Single CTA: call Jacob directly — no friction

SlimMaker — SureSlim® Launchboard

A digital launchboard for SureSlim®, connecting people to a medically founded, safer approach to weight loss — built to be the entry point for a broader health platform.

What We Built

SlimMaker needed a clean, conversion-focused landing experience that communicated medical credibility without feeling clinical. We built a modern static site that serves as the gateway into the SureSlim® ecosystem — clear value proposition, a strong call to action, and room to grow.

  • Medically-grounded messaging with accessible copy
  • Conversion-focused layout — hero, CTA, and "Find out more" flow
  • Built as a launchboard — designed to expand as the platform grows
  • Hosted on Moreland Made infrastructure (subdomain, edge-deployed)
